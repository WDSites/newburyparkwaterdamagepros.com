What happens when you call from outside Newbury Park

The process is the same in every town. You tell us where the water came from, what is wet and how long it has been there. We ask for the address, explain the first steps to take while you wait and describe what the job is likely to involve. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and same-day response is offered when available.

Drying and cleanup follow the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration as the industry reference, and S520 for mold. You can ask any company whether its crews work to them. We also tell you when a problem needs another trade, such as a plumber, a roofer or a drainage contractor.

Water providers across the Conejo Valley

Four water providers serve residents and businesses in the Conejo Valley. They are the City of Thousand Oaks, California American Water, California Water Service and Camrosa Water District, and the one that serves your street depends on the address. The City of Thousand Oaks publishes a Find Your Water Provider map, and your water bill names your provider. Knowing who to call for a main shutoff or a meter question saves time when water is moving.

Permit and building offices

Repair work after water damage can need a permit depending on scope, and the office that decides is the one for your city. For Newbury Park and Thousand Oaks addresses, call the City of Thousand Oaks building division at (805) 449-2320. For Moorpark, Building and Safety is at (805) 517-6272. For Camarillo, Westlake Village and Oak Park, ask your city building department before major work begins. Rules vary by scope, so ask before you start.

Which county and city rules apply

Utilities and permit offices change as you cross town lines. Newbury Park and Thousand Oaks addresses usually go through the City of Thousand Oaks, Camarillo and Moorpark have their own city offices, and Westlake Village and Oak Park sit in Los Angeles and Ventura County jurisdictions respectively. Ask your city building department before major repair work.
