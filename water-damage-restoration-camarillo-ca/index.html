Camarillo's rain season, and the dry months in between

What to do first in a Camarillo home

Shut off the water at the fixture or the main, keep people out of wet rooms and stay clear of outlets and the panel if water is near them. Photograph the damage, then call the line.

Camarillo gets about 12.01 inches of rain in a normal year, spread over roughly 36 days. That sounds small, but the town is flat, with Calleguas Creek watershed runoff moving across the eastern end of the Oxnard Plain. A single winter storm can leave water sitting against slab foundations and garage thresholds instead of draining away.

The long dry stretch causes a different problem. Soil and landscaping bake for months, then the first heavy rain hits hard ground and sheets off it toward the nearest low door. If your yard slopes toward the house, grade and gutter outlets are worth checking before October, not after.

Wildfire sits in that same cycle. The Camarillo Springs Fire of May 2013 burned about 28,000 acres and damaged about 15 houses, and overnight rain helped bring it under control by May 5. Burned slopes shed water faster, so rain after a fire is a real intrusion risk for homes near the hills.

Water is spreading through your Camarillo home

On the flat parts of town, water does not drain away. It travels sideways under baseboards, into closets and beneath flooring. A supply line failure at night can reach three rooms before anyone wakes up.

If water is still spreading

Stop the source

Shut the main valve or the fixture stop. If a storm is pushing water in, do not wade through it.

Check the panel first

Cut power only if the panel is reachable and dry. For any electrical or gas risk, call the utility or 911 before the restoration line.

Lift what you can

Pull rugs, books and electronics off the floor. Leave heavy furniture in place and put foil or plastic under the legs.

Keep it out of the street

Do not pump or hose dirty water into a storm drain. Contaminated water needs proper handling and disposal.

Photograph before cleanup

Take photos and video of the water line, damaged items and the source. This documentation helps with an insurance claim.

Camarillo risk factors we plan around

Flat plain drainage

Pleasant Valley sits at the eastern end of the Oxnard Plain at 177 feet. With so little slope, standing water lingers, so extraction and drying should start fast.

Post-fire rain

Homes near the Camarillo Hills and Santa Susana foothills can see faster runoff after a burn. Check door thresholds and vent openings before the first storm.

Retirement community units

Leisure Village opened in 1973. Older supply lines, water heaters and condensate lines in long-lived units are common leak sources.

Airport corridor buildings

Camarillo Airport sits about 3 miles west of the central business district. Commercial units there often have shared walls, so a leak can cross tenants.

What drives the cost of a Camarillo job

Cost depends on how much water, what kind, and how long it sat. Clean supply water caught early is the smallest job. Gray or sewage water, wet insulation, or water that has reached cabinets and subfloors raises the scope. We explain scope and price before work starts, and we do not quote prices over a photo.

Insurance varies by cause. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, so check your policy or ask your insurer. Standard homeowners' insurance does not cover flood damage, and the Camarillo flood risk on a low lot may call for a separate policy.

Permits and utilities in Camarillo

Camrosa Water District, headquartered on East Santa Rosa Road, serves nearly 30,000 people across Camarillo and the Santa Rosa Valley. Other small purveyors exist, so read your bill to confirm yours before calling about a shutoff or a meter.

Permit rules vary by scope. Replacing a water heater or repairing a water line can trigger one, while drywall repair may not. Ask the City of Camarillo building division before repair work begins. The neighboring towns work differently, so see our pages for Thousand Oaks and Moorpark if your property sits across a boundary.

Near CSU Channel Islands, on the former state hospital grounds, rental and campus-adjacent buildings often have a landlord or association to notify first. Do that early so access is arranged when the crew arrives.
