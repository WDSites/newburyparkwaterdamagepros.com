Water damage cleanup in Newbury Park, CA starts once the water is stopped. The job removes soaked carpet, pad, wet drywall and debris, then sanitizes and deodorizes what stays. The first 48 hours matter most, so if you are looking at wet materials now, call the 24/7 line, keep people and pets out of the wet area and keep power off in wet rooms only if the panel is dry to reach.

Safety steps before cleanup starts

Wear gloves and boots when handling wet material. Stay out of rooms where water reaches outlets or the panel. Do not mix household cleaners with sewage or floodwater, and bag soaked items instead of carrying them through clean areas.

Why the first 48 hours are an emergency

Wet porous materials start to break down quickly. Carpet pad holds water like a sponge, drywall wicks it upward, and odor begins to settle in. Getting soaked materials out in the first two days keeps the problem from growing and cuts down on what has to be replaced.

This page is about the cleanup step after the water source is shut off. If standing water is still on the floor, see emergency water removal first. If the walls are still wet, the drying side is covered under water mitigation.

How cleanup is done

Step 01

Assess the water category

Clean, gray or black water under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard decides what can be saved.

Step 02

Remove ruined materials

Wet carpet, pad, drywall and insulation are cut out and bagged for disposal.

Step 03

Sanitize

Antimicrobial treatment goes on framing and hard surfaces where the water category calls for it.

Step 04

Deodorize and verify

Odor is treated at the source and air is cleaned. Moisture readings confirm the area is ready for drying or repair.

Sanitizing and deodorizing

Sanitizing is about the surfaces left behind after removal. Framing, subfloors and concrete are cleaned, and an antimicrobial product goes on where the water category calls for it. For clean supply-line water this step is light. For gray or black water it is thorough and often done twice.

Deodorizing works on the source of the smell. Odor molecules settle into porous materials, so those materials are removed first. Air scrubbers and thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment may follow, depending on the room and the material. A smell that returns after drying usually means moisture is still present somewhere.

Two common mistakes make cleanup harder. One is drying a room with fans but leaving soaked pad underneath, which keeps the smell alive. The other is closing a wall over a damp cavity because the surface feels dry. Readings, not touch, decide when a space is ready.

Contents and belongings

Furniture and belongings that sat in water are sorted by what they are made of. Solid wood and hard goods can often be cleaned. Upholstery, mattresses and particleboard furniture are harder to save after contaminated water. Photograph everything before it moves, because those photos help with an insurance claim.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle water damage cleanup emergencies at night or on weekends in Newbury Park?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open every day, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day response is offered when available.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Sometimes. Carpet soaked by clean water and extracted quickly may be saved, but pad is usually replaced. Carpet touched by gray or black water is usually removed.

How do you get rid of the damp smell?

Odor comes from wet materials, so the source is removed first. Sanitizing, air cleaning and drying then control what is left.

What should I photograph before cleanup starts?

Photograph the water, the damaged materials, the affected rooms and any damaged belongings. Keep receipts for any emergency purchases.

Do I need to get a permit for cleanup?

Cleanup alone often does not, but permit rules vary by scope. Ask your city building department before repair work.
