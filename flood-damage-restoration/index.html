Flood damage restoration in Newbury Park, CA covers water that comes in from outside or from a sewer line, as opposed to a clean pipe leak. If floodwater is entering your home, get people and pets to higher ground, stay away from electrical panels and outlets, and call the 24/7 line. Do not walk through standing water until you know where the power is.

When floodwater is an emergency

Floodwater is an emergency from the first inch. It can carry silt, bacteria and chemicals, and it can reach outlets, furnaces and water heaters. Water that rose from the ground or from a drain is treated as contaminated, which changes what can be kept and what has to be removed.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard groups water by category. Category 1 is clean supply water, Category 2 is gray water with some contamination, and Category 3 is black water from sewage or outside flooding. Floods are usually Category 2 or 3, which is why porous materials like carpet and pad are often removed.

Safety first

If water has reached the electrical panel, outlets or a gas appliance, stay out. Cut power only if the panel can be reached completely dry. Call your electric or gas utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk before you call the restoration line. Do not use household cleaners to mix with sewage or floodwater.

How a flood job runs

Step 01

Make it safe

Power and gas risks are checked, and the crew protects itself with gloves, boots and respirators when the water is contaminated.

Step 02

Extract and remove

Pumps and extraction units pull the water out. Soaked carpet, pad and wet drywall that cannot be saved are removed.

Step 03

Clean and sanitize

Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned, and antimicrobial treatment is applied where the water category calls for it.

Step 04

Dry and verify

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings confirm it before repair begins.

Basement and lower-level flooding cleanup

If your home has a basement, a finished lower level or a below-grade room, flooding there is a different job than a flooded living room. Water sits low and has nowhere to drain, so pumps and extraction are the first step. Carpet and pad come up, wet drywall is cut back above the waterline, and the slab or subfloor is dried and cleaned.

Find out where the water came from before you decide what to remove. A broken supply line gives Category 1 water, groundwater seepage is usually Category 2 or 3, and a sewer backup is always Category 3. Move belongings to dry ground, but do not carry soaked items through the house without protection.

Restoration is not waterproofing. If the water keeps returning, a drainage or foundation contractor is the right trade, and that is a separate job. We restore the damage and tell you honestly when the cause needs another specialist.

Sewage backup cleanup

Raw sewage from a floor drain or toilet is a Category 3 event. Stay out of the area, keep children and pets away, and call. Do not try to clean it up with a mop or household products, because that spreads contamination through the house.

The crew extracts the sewage, removes carpet, pad and porous materials the sewage touched, and cleans and sanitizes hard surfaces. Affected drywall is cut out. The area is dried and treated before anything is rebuilt. If the backup came from a city sewer line, tell your sewer provider as well.

Flood risk and insurance in Newbury Park

Arroyo Conejo flows southwest through Newbury Park, and a flood-control channel runs along the US 101 corridor. Look up your address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center to see whether your home is mapped as a flood hazard area, and ask your city building department about local drainage.

According to the California Department of Insurance, standard homeowners insurance does not cover flood damage. Flood coverage usually comes from a separate National Flood Insurance Program policy, which generally takes effect 30 days after purchase. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep photos and a written scope.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle flood emergencies at night or on weekends in Newbury Park?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered at night, on weekends and on holidays. Same-day response is offered when available.

Is floodwater safe to clean up myself?

Floodwater and sewage can carry bacteria and may touch live electrical circuits. Stay out until the power is safe and call for help with anything beyond a small clean spill.

Will flood damage be covered by my homeowner policy?

Standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood, according to the California Department of Insurance. Flood coverage usually requires a separate policy. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

What is the difference between flood restoration and waterproofing?

Restoration removes water, cleans and dries what was damaged. Waterproofing is a drainage or foundation fix that keeps water out. If the problem returns, a waterproofing contractor may be needed.

How do I check whether my home is in a flood zone?

Use FEMA's Flood Map Service Center and enter your address. You can also ask your city building department or your insurance agent.
